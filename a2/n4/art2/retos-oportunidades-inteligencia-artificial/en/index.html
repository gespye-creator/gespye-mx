Abstract.

Artificial Intelligence has become a key tool in the Mexican business environment, especially where financial, tax, and legal activities, processes, and operations are simplified. Optimizing to reduce workload is the way to address technological advancement's challenges and opportunities, reduce costs, and streamline administrative management. The research objective is to analyze the impact of artificial intelligence on four companies in the Guadalajara Metropolitan Area, Jalisco, as well as their challenges and opportunities in the face of technological advancement for cost reduction and efficient administrative management. The qualitative approach is based on documentary analysis and observation. Historical, descriptive, explanatory, and correlational approaches to identify its impact as a driver of economic and social transformation. The conclusion is that it is a highly relevant multidisciplinary tool, with rapid and efficient access to information, including risks, threats, and opportunities for growth.

Keywords:

Artificial intelligence, foresight, business sector

Resumen.

La Inteligencia Artificial se ha convertido en una herramienta clave en el entorno empresarial mexicano, especial en áreas en donde, se simplifican actividades, procesos y operaciones financieras, fiscales, legales, entre otras. Optimizar para disminuir carga laboral es la manera de enfrentar retos y oportunidades ante el avance tecnológico, con la finalidad de reducir costos y eficientes la gestión administrativa. El objetivo de investigación es: analizar el impacto de la Inteligencia Artificial en cuatro empresas de la Zona Metropolitana de Guadalajara, Jalisco; sus retos y oportunidades ante el avance tecnológico, para la reducción de costos y la eficiente gestión administrativa. Con enfoque cualitativo, basado en análisis documental y la observación. Del tipo histórico, descriptivo, explicativo y correlacional para identificar su impacto como un motor de transformación económica y social. Se concluye que es una herramienta multidisciplinaria de gran relevancia, con acceso a la información de manera rápida y eficiente; con riesgos, amenazas y oportunidades de crecimiento.

Introduction

Artificial Intelligence (AI) has evolved significantly since its origin in the 1950s, becoming a key tool in optimizing business processes. Its implementation improves efficiency, automates tasks, and enables data-driven strategic decision-making, although it also faces important ethical, technical, and regulatory challenges. The starting point was in 1956, the year of the Dartmouth College Conference in New Hampshire (USA), where the term was used for the first time to refer to a new field of knowledge, according to Castro and Ferreira (2020).

The concept of AI was introduced by Alan Turing and formalized by John McCarthy in 1956. Its development has gone through various stages, from expert systems to machine learning. In the business context, it has transformed sectors such as supply chain management, marketing, and finance, optimizing processes and enhancing competitiveness. Companies like IBM, NVIDIA, and Google have driven its advancement through strategic collaborations across various industries to optimize workflows with AI, as mentioned by (Blaine, 2024).

The growing use of AI raises questions regarding its impact on organizations. While this technology offers major advantages, such as reducing time and costs, it also presents significant challenges, including the need to train personnel and assess risks associated with programming errors or data bias. Therefore, in this research, the general question is: What is the impact of Artificial Intelligence on four companies in the Guadalajara Metropolitan Area, Jalisco; their challenges and opportunities in the face of technological advancement, for cost reduction and efficient administrative management?

The general objective is: to analyze the impact of Artificial Intelligence on four companies in the Guadalajara Metropolitan Area, Jalisco; their challenges and opportunities in the face of technological advancement, for cost reduction and efficient administrative management. As a tentative answer to the general research question, the following alternative hypothesis is presented: by identifying the impact of Artificial Intelligence on four companies in the Guadalajara Metropolitan Area, Jalisco, challenges and opportunities in the face of technological advancement are established, thereby reducing costs and making administrative management efficient.

The research is considered relevant because the use of AI has grown significantly in Mexico, especially after the COVID-19 pandemic, increasing by 40% in companies. Its adoption has improved productivity, customer service, and decision-making, consolidating itself as a strategic resource for business growth. To support this research, three variables are considered:

1. Mexican business sector (independent);

2. Artificial Intelligence (dependent 1); and

3. Challenges and opportunities in the face of technological advancement (dependent 2).

Through these, a historical framework with 11 authors, a theoretical framework with 7, and the state of the art with 9 are built. The methodology used follows a qualitative approach, supported by observation and documentary review; it is exploratory, descriptive, correlational, and applied in nature, with a non-experimental, cross-sectional design. The results indicate that Artificial Intelligence is an important tool that should be leveraged in the Mexican business sector; its use requires constant updating, effort, objectivity, and willingness for its optimal utilization.

It is concluded that AI is within everyone's reach through a computer, mobile device, video games, virtual assistant, among others, with significant implementation challenges that must be considered, relating to ethics and values such as responsibility, respect, tolerance, empathy, and willingness. The objective of analyzing the impact of AI on the Mexican business sector is fulfilled; consequently, the general research question is answered, and the hypothesis is accepted upon identifying the impact on organizations.

Historical Framework

The concept of artificial intelligence (AI) became known in the 1950s with various authors, most notably Alan Turing and Marvin Minsky, as pioneers of the theoretical and technological foundations. In 2010, development and adoption accelerated with machine learning to process massive data and identify patterns, among other capabilities (Russell & Norvig, 2021).

Only through this historical understanding can the true magnitude of its impact and the possibilities it offers to transform business processes be appreciated. Artificial Intelligence (AI) emerged as a concept when Alan M. Turing published his influential article titled "Computing Machinery and Intelligence" in the journal Mind (Iberdrola, 2025).

Its interpretation varies across disciplines, but generally speaking, it refers to the ability of machines to perform tasks that require human intelligence, such as reasoning, learning, and decision-making. Since AI encompasses a broad spectrum of technologies and applications, its deep and effective understanding within the business environment requires a detailed analysis of its origins and evolution. It is imperative to know the early developments and advances in this field, as well as the fundamental ideas that gave rise to its expansion and adoption across various industries.

In his work, Turing introduced "the idea of artificial intelligence and presented what is now known as the Turing Test, a key methodology to determine whether a machine can simulate intelligent behavior according to human standards" (Niebla Zatarain, et al., 2024, pp. 176-179).

This concept marked an evolution in the field, offering a way to evaluate whether a machine could exhibit behaviors considered typical and intelligent. However, during the 1960s, artificial intelligence did not receive the attention it deserved due to the high investment required for its development and application. It was not until the 1970s and 1980s that significant progress was made in Expert Systems, a branch of AI that enabled the resolution of complex problems through the use of specialized knowledge. "These programs imitated the decision-making process of human experts" (Serrahima de Bedoya & Álvaro, 2022, pp. 13-14).

Theoretical Framework

The growing digitization and competitiveness of business environments have driven interest in the study of artificial intelligence (AI) in business administration. Its implementation has generated significant transformations, particularly in process automation and the optimization of resource utilization. These technologies enable organizations to adopt more agile and effective strategies, thereby improving operational efficiency (Fontenla-Romero & Calvo-Rolle, 2018).

Artificial intelligence has been defined as a set of technological systems capable of simulating human reasoning in performing tasks, with the ability to learn and progressively improve from the data they process (OECD, 2019). In the European regulatory context, it is understood as a machine-operated system that can operate with varying levels of autonomy and adapt after deployment; it uses input data to generate outputs such as recommendations, predictions, or decisions that impact physical or digital environments (European Parliament and Council of the EU, 2024).

Various studies have shown that intelligent systems enable more accurate and agile data-driven decision-making. This fosters innovation in areas such as supply chain management, logistics optimization, and customer satisfaction. The ability of these systems to analyze large volumes of information in real time also allows for pattern identification and market trend anticipation (Meuter et al., 2005).

In the financial field, AI has profoundly transformed how organizations manage operations. Through machine learning algorithms, it is possible to detect fraud early, optimize investment portfolios, and develop personalized customer service systems (Fontenla-Romero & Calvo-Rolle, 2018). Furthermore, real-time data analysis has improved risk management strategies, helping anticipate and mitigate potential vulnerabilities in volatile markets.

However, adopting these technologies presents significant challenges, such as the need to establish adequate regulatory frameworks, ensure algorithmic transparency, and address ethical implications. These elements are crucial for preserving trust among investors and financial system users (European Parliament and Council of the EU, 2024).

In human resources, AI has redefined traditional processes. Through task automation such as resume filtering or preliminary interviews via chatbots, companies have achieved greater efficiency and precision in recruitment processes. For example, when receiving a large number of applications, an organization can use AI to select suitable profiles based on defined criteria and conduct basic automated interviews that assess aspects like availability, commitment, and communication skills. This not only reduces hiring time and costs, but also enables HR personnel to focus on strategic activities such as training planning and organizational culture enhancement.

The rapid development of AI and robotics, driven by advances in algorithms and technological systems, is revolutionizing traditional business models. Companies apply these technologies not only to optimize logistics, but also to innovate in digital marketing, analytical accounting, and talent management, generating a substantial improvement in operational efficiency (Fontenla-Romero & Calvo-Rolle, 2018).

Collaborative and partnership strategies between companies have been key in driving advanced AI-based solutions. For instance, IBM Watson Health and Guerbet developed a joint solution to improve cancer diagnosis using artificial intelligence, demonstrating its potential in the healthcare sector (Consalud.es, 2019). In the automotive sector, the alliance between NVIDIA and Mercedes-Benz has enabled AI implementation in autonomous vehicles, marking a milestone in mobility transformation; beyond technology, innovation has been key to creating attractive market designs (Xataka, 2021).

It is important to note that artificial intelligence does not replace the human factor; rather, it complements it by freeing up time and resources for higher strategic value activities. By facilitating agile, data-grounded decision-making, these technologies become essential allies in a dynamic and constantly evolving business environment (OECD, 2019).

State of the Art

Artificial intelligence (AI) has ceased to be an experimental tool to become a key component of digital transformation in organizations. Its evolution has been driven by advances in machine learning, big data processing, deep neural network development, and the availability of powerful computing resources (Russell & Norvig, 2021). These advances have enabled its application in areas such as process automation, decision-making enhancement, service personalization, and resource optimization.

According to Grand View Research (2024), the global artificial intelligence market will reach an estimated value of $279.22 billion in 2024, with a projected compound annual growth rate of 35.9% from 2025 to 2030.

In the organizational context, AI contributes to improving operational efficiency through routine task automation, predictive analytics, and real-time data management. It also facilitates the design of efficient, evidence-based business strategies (Davenport & Ronanki, 2018). However, implementing these technologies is not without challenges. Key challenges include resistance to change from human capital, lack of adequate technological infrastructure, and concerns regarding the ethical use of algorithms and personal data (Jobin, Ienca & Vayena, 2019).

Several studies have noted that while AI can improve productivity, it also poses significant implications for employment. Automation threatens to displace workers with repetitive or easily programmable tasks, while increasing demand for profiles with advanced digital skills (Brynjolfsson & McAfee, 2017). This has led to a growing need for workforce reskilling programs and public policies aimed at a fair and inclusive digital transition.

Despite technological progress, De la Torre et al. (2025) point out that the digital divide remains a significant challenge. By 2024, connectivity, internet access, and mobile device usage highlighted social and economic inequality in resource-limited regions. They also emphasize that companies must recognize digital literacy as a long-term strategy for success, wherein technical skills and employee training are treated as an investment that translates into efficiency and innovation. A resilient, socially responsible organization evolves into a modern entity that fosters skills for employee development, business growth, and inclusion in an increasingly digitized society.

In the Latin American context, Guillermo Coronel (2025) mentions that the Economic Commission for Latin America and the Caribbean (ECLAC) noted in 2022 that AI development in businesses is still nascent, with strong reliance on imported technologies and limited investment in local research. In a report requested by the National Alliance for Artificial Intelligence (ANIA) to UNESCO for a diagnostic of AI development in Mexico, it is mentioned that despite the absence of a regulating authority, various public sector entities are incorporating AI into daily operations; however, Mexico scores very low on institutional use and current strategy management, with no public participation accounted for.

In sum, current literature shows that AI represents a tool with enormous transformative potential for organizations. However, effective adoption requires more than technological investment: it demands a comprehensive approach considering ethical, social, regulatory, and talent development dimensions. Exploring these areas represents a valuable contribution to developing sustainable organizational transformation models.

Research Approach

Artificial Intelligence (AI) in the business sector is a progressive topic; its growing acceptance and interest due to its impact on efficiency, decision-making, and market competitiveness make it increasingly used. To better understand the subject, a qualitative approach based on documentary analysis and observation was utilized, through which AI implementation was analyzed using non-numerical data such as interviews, document reviews, and observations; the goal was to gain a deep understanding of how AI transforms processes, decision-making, and interactions among various stakeholders.

Explorations were conducted across different companies, incorporating feedback from operational managers who deploy AI and implement workplace processes. The information obtained allowed for the identification of challenges, opportunities, and resistance to changes implemented within organizations. Once interviews were conducted, the impact generated through the variety of applications used by each firm was analyzed, identifying technological requirements across functions such as customer service, social media management, marketing, human talent, among others. Findings are presented in the results section; names of companies and individuals are omitted for data privacy.

Type of Research

The research type is historical, examining the origin of AI, its incorporation into companies, and its evolution over time, identifying authors who highlight the topic's importance and long-term vision. It is exploratory and descriptive, applied throughout the document as existing literature was explored for historical, theoretical, and state-of-the-art frameworks, describing findings to support this study.

It is correlational and applied, analyzing variables—both Artificial Intelligence and the Mexican business sector—to identify challenges and opportunities in the face of technological advancement. This was accomplished through literature review and interviews with individuals who constantly interact with available tools, both paid and free.

AI implementation in the business sector was analyzed using non-numerical data from interviews, documentary analysis, and observation. Through this, a deep understanding of how AI transforms processes, decision-making, and stakeholder interaction was sought, capturing perceptions, experiences, and opinions of experts, managers, and employees regarding workplace implementation and use.

Research Design

A non-experimental, cross-sectional design was used, gathering information during interviews with each manager at a single point in time; notes were taken to precisely describe the collected information without alteration.

Results

To identify the impact of AI with its array of market applications and tools, interviews were conducted during February, March, and April 2025 with operational managers from four companies in the Guadalajara Metropolitan Area, Jalisco. These companies were selected based on their business sector, location, and willingness to provide information to analyze impact, technological challenges and opportunities, cost effects, and administrative management impact. For the purposes of this document, companies are designated as A, B, C, and D to ensure data privacy and protect the reputation and integrity of both organizations and interviewees; appreciation is extended for their willingness and the information gathered.

Interviews with operational AI leads across different departments identified a variety of applications suited for customer service, operations, marketing, among others. Conversational assistants emerged as the most widely used tool, accounting for 29% of user volume, which directly impacts operational costs, as affirmed by Company "A". Here, automation reduced the workforce from 30 employees to four who currently handle application maintenance, updates, and analysis. Aiming to reach 40% cost optimization, managers noted that the drawback lies in spending less time on direct communication, which limits timely identification of grievances; this poses a risk of customer loss and impacts administrative efficiency.

To mitigate these risks, the company implemented customer service via a chat interface powered by OpenAI technology for efficient response generation, providing predictive suggestions, grammar corrections, and message consistency. This standardizes service quality and optimizes response time, leading to a high perceived influence of AI.

At Company "B", the analysis focused on recruitment processes, provided by the operational lead. Benefits, limitations, and effects on cost reduction, management efficiency, and process definition were identified. This company uses an external tool that filters information from multiple web sources, yielding highly specific results aligned with set parameters. Under this system, four employees collectively manage human talent tasks to identify ideal candidate profiles.

The tool is also used for job descriptions and vacancies, optimizing posting, interviewing, and hiring times. Nevertheless, a major challenge lies in reviewing generated text to ensure a human touch, clear and precise phrasing, and reader trust. Initially, resumes are screened using AI; while efficient, limitations such as spelling errors, impractical formatting, inappropriate imagery unaligned with intended messaging, and data misinterpretation were identified. Consequently, suitable candidates were automatically rejected. In this case, although the impact is positive, it remains insufficient for cost reduction and inefficient for administrative management until identified deficiencies and limitations are resolved.

The third company, Company "C", uses AI through a custom application designed for commercial marketing aimed at streamlining processes, broadening market reach, positioning the firm as innovative and cutting-edge, and establishing its market identity as a strategic marketing player using smart systems and real projects tailored to client needs with fast, efficient, and impactful solutions.

The tool's impact is positive: tasks are automated, data is managed, trends are analyzed, strategies are proposed, business inquiries are resolved, administrative processes are defined, high-value commercial systems are created, project specifications are refined, and market needs are identified while ensuring organizational efficiency. Consequently, costs are reduced and administrative management is deemed efficient.

Company "D", dedicated to audience ad view analysis, leverages technological advancement to position itself in the market through efficiency and competitiveness. An interview was conducted with a marketer with eight years of experience in image and project design, who noted that AI accelerates both administrative and operational tasks. Process automation, management, and retention position them as reputable, professional, and innovative leaders.

Beyond design, image creation, projects, and content creation, the tool's impact is reflected in administrative efficiency and sales. By automating tasks, experts focus on complex case analysis, positioning AI as a facilitator that optimizes time, reduces costs, and maximizes human and technological resources; thus, its impact is considered high for this company.

Conclusions

It is concluded that among the four companies interviewed in the Guadalajara Metropolitan Area, Jalisco, three (A, C, and D) demonstrate a very high impact from Artificial Intelligence, where technological challenges and opportunities translate into cost reduction and efficient administrative management. Meanwhile, for Company "B", although AI influence is high, identifying root causes, consequences, and solutions is required to achieve a high real impact.

Therefore, the main objective is fulfilled by analyzing the impact of Artificial Intelligence on four companies in the Guadalajara Metropolitan Area, Jalisco; their challenges and opportunities in the face of technological advancement for cost reduction and efficient administrative management. Consequently, the research question is answered and the alternative hypothesis is accepted, confirming that identifying AI's impact in these companies establishes technological challenges and opportunities, thereby reducing costs and streamlining administrative management.

Overall, as Artificial Intelligence becomes an increasingly prevalent tool across personal, professional, and enterprise domains, analyzing the daily challenges, benefits, and hurdles of technological evolution is imperative. Governments must urgently design public policies and regulatory frameworks that safeguard progress, sustainable development, equity, rights, ethics, respect, human dignity, and the common good.

Transversality reveals broad benefits; however, risks regarding ethics, values, responsibility, employment, education, security, and equality cannot be overlooked. Providing access via mobile devices exposes personal data and information, potentially compromising rights and freedoms—topics to be developed in future work.
