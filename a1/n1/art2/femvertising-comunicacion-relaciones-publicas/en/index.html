Abstract.

In the fight for gender equality, femvertising, or pro-women's advertising, has emerged as a powerful tool in communication and public relations to challenge gender stereotypes and promote equality, playing a crucial role in women's empowerment. Organizations have found it necessary to transform the way they communicate with female audiences. This paper examines the impact of femvertising on the communication and public relations strategies of organizations, highlighting its influence on the perception of female consumers and the promotion of gender equity in the media. It also analyzes the challenges and opportunities that organizations face when implementing femvertising strategies.

Keywords:

Femvertising, Communication, Public relations, Organizations, Women's empowerment.

Introduction

Communication and public relations are fundamental elements in the contemporary business environment, where building a solid brand image and connecting with the audience are imperatives for organizational success. In addition to being powerful tools to give voice and visibility to women. In this context, the phenomenon of femvertising has emerged as a disruptive force that is radically transforming how organizations design their communication and public relations strategies. The concept of femvertising arises from the fusion of the terms "feminism" and "advertising" (Becker-Herby, 2016; Mora, 2018; Varghese, 2020) and refers to advertising that promotes messages of female empowerment, challenging traditional gender stereotypes and promoting gender equality. In the 21st century, gender equality remains a fundamental objective on the global agenda, and the phenomenon of femvertising has emerged as an innovative strategy in the field of communication and public relations.

The impact of femvertising on the communication and public relations strategy of organizations is undeniable and multifaceted. First, femvertising has allowed brands to connect in a more authentic and meaningful way with their female audience. In a world where women increasingly demand equitable representation in the media and advertising, brands that adopt this strategy can establish a deeper emotional bond with them by presenting them in diverse and empowered roles in their advertising campaigns. Femvertising has revolutionized how brands communicate with women and how women perceive their own power and capacity to influence the world around them. This authentic approach not only generates greater brand affinity, but also fosters greater engagement and loyalty on the part of female consumers.

Furthermore, femvertising has generated important conversations in society on issues related to gender equality. By addressing topics such as the representation of women in the media, the gender pay gap, and the fight against sexism, these advertising campaigns have contributed to raising public awareness and promoting positive cultural change. Femvertising not only sells products, but also promotes values and principles that reflect a more inclusive and equitable vision of society. In this sense, organizations that adopt femvertising are not only promoting their products or services, but are also contributing to broader and more meaningful social change.

Femvertising

For decades, advertising has perpetuated gender stereotypes and limited the representation of women to traditional and stereotyped roles. Femvertising, or advertising that promotes messages of female empowerment, arises in response to the demand for a more authentic, inclusive, equitable, and positive representation of women in advertising and media. In light of these facts, brands have recognized the importance of aligning with values of equity and empowerment.

This advertising strategy seeks to challenge traditional gender stereotypes and promote messages that celebrate the diversity, equality, strength, empowerment, and autonomy of women. Through inspiring narratives and authentic representations of women, it becomes an effective alternative for brands wishing to connect with the female audience in an authentic and meaningful way (Menéndez, 2019; Hernández-Willoughby, 2021). Furthermore, it contributes to building a more inclusive and equitable culture by encouraging changes in attitudes and behaviors toward women in daily life.

Femvertising employs strategies to effectively transmit messages of female empowerment. This may include representing women in non-traditional roles and incorporating topics relevant to women in contemporary society, such as gender equality, self-acceptance, and female leadership. This has transformed how brands relate to women and how women perceive their own power and role in society, positively influencing women's purchasing decisions as they identify with brands that share their values of equality and empowerment (Feiereisen et al., 2009; Lanuza, 2019; Hernández-Willoughby, 2021). This advertising strategy seeks not only to sell products, but also to inspire women to feel more self-confident and empowered to pursue their personal and professional aspirations and goals.

Impact on Communication and Public Relations Strategies

The phenomenon of femvertising has had a significant impact on the communication and public relations strategies of organizations around the world. This advertising contributes to how brands communicate with their audience, especially with women, connecting with consumers who value authenticity and inclusion, allowing them to differentiate themselves in a saturated market. Femvertising has generated important conversations in society and contributed to building a more inclusive and equitable culture. In addition, it has proven to be an effective tool for driving consumer engagement and strengthening brand identity, becoming a valuable avenue to connect with the target audience (Case, 2019). This advertising strategy has gained relevance in recent years as a tool to connect with an increasingly conscious and demanding public regarding gender equality and representation; it has redefined how organizations design their communication and public relations strategies, encouraging them to adopt a more inclusive approach committed to promoting women's empowerment.

Consumer Perception

Consumer perception of the femvertising phenomenon is a crucial aspect in studying how these advertising campaigns impact the communication and public relations strategy of organizations. Overall, consumer perception of femvertising tends to be positive. These advertising campaigns can generate an emotional and empathetic response from women, as they see their experiences and aspirations reflected in the messages promoted by brands (Becker-Herby, 2016; Åkestam et al., 2017; Hernández-Willoughby, 2021). The representation of strong, independent, and diverse women can contribute to reinforcing consumers' self-esteem and identity, as well as generating a sense of connection with the brand.

In addition, femvertising can be perceived as a positive signal of a brand's commitment to gender equality and female empowerment. Consumers increasingly value authenticity and corporate social responsibility from brands, and femvertising can be seen as a demonstration of such commitment. Brands that adopt this strategy can build a more positive and attractive image for consumers, translating into greater loyalty and preference from this market segment (Hernández-Willoughby, 2021). However, it is also important to recognize that consumer perception of femvertising can vary depending on various factors, such as cultural context, campaign authenticity, and consistency with brand values. Some consumers may be critical of femvertising if they perceive that brands are capitalizing on the feminist movement for purely commercial purposes without a genuine commitment to gender equality (Baxter, 2015; Menéndez, 2019a; Martell, 2019). Therefore, authenticity and consistency in the implementation of femvertising are key to maintaining a positive perception among consumers.

Femvertising can contribute to building a solid brand and a positive perception among consumers, who state that they feel most identified with advertising that reflects the reality of their experiences and moves away from traditional roles and stereotypes (Lanuza, 2019; Hernández-Willoughby, 2021). Femvertising campaigns that promote values of equality and female empowerment can resonate with audiences, generating an emotional connection and fostering brand loyalty. Furthermore, femvertising can help organizations position themselves as leaders in promoting gender equity and diversity, which can be an important factor for consumers.

Challenges and Opportunities

Although femvertising offers numerous opportunities for organizations and is considered a powerful tool for promoting women's empowerment, it also faces significant challenges that must be addressed to maximize its effectiveness. One of its main challenges arises from its comparison with "commodity feminism" or commercial feminism, where messages are constructed from the fusion of gender stereotypes and feminist ideals with a purely commercial objective (Menéndez, 2019b). Being perceived as marketing strategies disguised as women's empowerment that do not truly address systemic issues of gender inequality (Franco et al., 2022). Achieving campaigns that are authentic and do not end up being perceived as simple attempts to capitalize on the feminist movement for commercial purposes represents a challenge for organizations, which must ensure that their actions and values match their messages and truly reflect a commitment to gender equality.

Another major concern is ensuring that femvertising campaigns represent the experiences and perspectives of all women. The lack of diversity in representations can lead to criticism for perpetuating stereotypes or ignoring certain groups of women. Organizations must be prepared to handle criticism and respond in an appropriate and transparent manner to concerns raised by the public.

On the other hand, there are great opportunities to generate positive change through inclusive communication strategies and alliances between organizations committed to gender equality. Femvertising provides a space to foster women's empowerment and can become a powerful tool to educate and raise awareness about gender issues that promote positive change in society.

Conclusions

The impact of femvertising on organizational communication and public relations is profound and significant. This form of advertising has proven to be a powerful tool for connecting with the audience, generating important conversations, and promoting positive cultural change. Through femvertising, organizations can establish a deeper emotional connection with their female audience and demonstrate their commitment to gender equality, women's empowerment, and inclusive representation. To maximize the impact of femvertising on communication and public relations, organizations must ensure that their campaigns genuinely reflect their values and commitments, backing them with concrete corporate social responsibility actions. By playing a crucial role in building a more equitable and fair culture, femvertising has the potential to generate a significant impact on society. However, it is important for organizations to proactively and transparently address the challenges and criticisms that may arise, committing to continuously improving their gender and diversity practices and policies.
