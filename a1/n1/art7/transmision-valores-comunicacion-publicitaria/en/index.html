The importance of transmitting values in the advertising communication of sports organizations and the impact on their audiences

La importancia de la transmisión de valores en la Comunicación Publicitaria de las Organizaciones Deportivas y el impacto en sus públicos

Abstract.

The present document aims to explain the importance of transmitting values in sports organizations and its impact on the behavior of their audiences. Communication and public relations play a fundamental role in the smooth operation of both internal and external aspects of these organizations. The bond that a sports organization can create by transmitting strategic messages based on values is to enhance the interest of its various audiences. Values, as a cognitive factor within the advertising, marketing, and public relations communications of sports organizations, can generate a positive impact when recognized by their audiences. In this way, sports organizations can strengthen their position with their audiences and have a more significant impact both socially and economically.

Keywords:

Values; Sports Organizations; Communication; Public Relations.

Introduction

Sports organizations have the need to communicate in order to transmit messages with the intention of connecting with their environment and achieving actions that contribute to the fulfillment of their goals and objectives. The application of different strategies to increase organizational benefits has turned communication into an effective tool for consolidating economic sports initiatives (Arnott, 2011).

On the other hand, values are axes of human behavior that can be identified and applied both positively and negatively. From a human cognitive perspective, the transmission of values represents beliefs and principles that lead people to carry out actions with awareness, morality, and ethics. The importance of the impact of values in the sports industry is essential in physical activity practice, spectator sports, and sports organizations alike. The relationship between values and sports organizations can satisfactorily impact various economic, social, and sporting benefits. By transmitting values through strategic communication, sports organizations can connect with their internal and external audiences, generating an impact through actions that benefit the organization.

Communication in Sports Organizations

Communication within sports organizations is becoming increasingly relevant in strategies aimed at establishing direct or indirect connections with their audiences to achieve goals and objectives effectively. As Canel (2007) mentions, “Communication is at the center of every strategic conception: it assists in achieving objectives, helps express the organization's position, and turns organizational managers into genuine agents of change” (p. 80).

Managing communication and its strategies along with the message to be transmitted internally and externally in a sports organization is essential for building connections with its audiences, thereby achieving various goals such as the sale of products, services, and events offered by the organizations. The importance of communication is crucial for short-, medium-, and long-term growth in sports organizations. According to literature, the communication department has evolved the most within football clubs, surpassing areas such as sports management and finance (Gómez et al., 2008). This can be exemplified as follows: if a sports organization (football club) fails to transmit effective communication, its audiences will likely misinterpret the information and not perform the action desired by the organization. This can lead to lower stadium attendance, fewer purchases of products and services, and a reduced sense of identity and belonging among fans. Enhancing relationships with target audiences of sports organizations can build a bond of identity, achieving benefits for both.

The management of communication within sports organizations must consider the five principles mentioned by Henderson (2001): 1) Managing the overabundance of information generated by sports that is channeled through various media platforms; 2) Controlling news overexposure to react proactively; 3) Establishing communicative strategies in response to changes occurring in society and the business interests surrounding the sports world; 4) Communicatively managing the internationalization of sports organizations in a highly globalized world; and 5) Remaining attentive to shifts in public opinion regarding the image of sports organizations, especially during crisis situations. Among these principles, number three stands out, as it relates to the impact that an effective message can create, given that the social context surrounding a sports organization can influence how audiences receive messages. One of the most important tasks for communication departments in football clubs is the effectiveness of fan communication and how it influences the consumption of offered products and services (Summers & Morgan, 2008).

The Importance of Audiences in Sports Organizations

Over time, sports organizations have faced the need for professionalization due to sports as entertainment generating media impact and economic interests. For instance, football clubs have begun developing various activities, most notably commercial ones aimed primarily at generating economic benefits for the organization.

According to Urrutia (2006), there are four key reasons why society shows interest in sports organizations: the entertainment potential offered to the public, serving as a reference for exemplary figures due to the association with the positive values of sport, the potential to integrate and identify audiences of diverse characteristics and nationalities—whether individual or collective (cities, communities, or even countries)—and the financial sums and interests involved. This dynamic generates attraction and interest among various stakeholders in the sports industry, such as government entities, federations, competition organizations or leagues, associations, media, sponsors, businesses, and fans. The same author refers to the virtuous circle that can be formed between audiences and organizations when a positive relationship is established—a virtuous circle that can boost commercial, economic, social, and sporting benefits.

Part of the communication and public relations strategies used by sports organizations for marketing products and services involves identifying their audiences. Communication strategies achieve their main purpose by understanding audience needs and, based on that, crafting messages and selecting media channels to ensure effective communication. In doing so, sports organizations ensure better message perception and a stronger intent to act from their audiences. According to Oliveira, Capriotti & Matilla (2014), identifying audiences in communication and public relations campaigns is mandatory due to their diverse characteristics and needs when perceiving communication, emphasizing the importance of tailoring messages and channels for greater impact.

Transmitting graphic, informative, visual, and oral messages within social sciences—specifically across communication, public relations, advertising, and marketing—requires essential elements such as the source, channel, medium, and receiver as the final destination. Hence, identifying to whom and how messages are directed is crucial. For Borges Virginia, defining the audience to whom communications are directed within their social and cultural contexts is key to increasing the likelihood of product or service purchases (Borges, 2012). On the other hand, Reinares and Ponzoa emphasize the importance of identifying audiences to build a stable relationship, enabling communication exchange and meeting their needs while ensuring a state of trust (Reinares & Ponzoa, 2004).

One of the most important and key links to achieving a direct connection between audiences and sports organizations is identity and cultural factors. As Colbert and Cuadrado (2003) point out, creating a shared culture between the sports club or organization and consumers—incorporating sporting and social values—is essential.

The Influence of Values on the Communication of Sports Organizations

The influence of values on changing human behavior and attitudes has been studied mostly in psychology due to the cognitive and emotional connotations they evoke in people. Values form part of human beliefs and principles, shaping the behavioral foundation for decision-making across various social contexts. From a communication perspective, transmitting values can persuade receivers and bring about attitudinal changes. According to Romero Sepúlveda (2021), incorporating values into sports organization communication creates a bond with the community and enhances interest in their products and services. The strategy of linking values, sports organizations, and audiences can strengthen identity and mutual benefits (Romero, 2021). We agree with Castelló-Martínez and Pino (2015) that shared values between an organization and an audience generate and consolidate identification and a sense of belonging attributed to brand products and services.

Bhattacharya and Sen (2003) as well as Marín and Ruiz (2007) state that consumers relate to companies cognitively, creating a connection that identifies them with one another by sharing traits, values, or attributes, influencing affective and behavioral states toward the company. In her research, Kristof (1996) proposes four key connections: values, shared personality traits, common goals, and individual need satisfaction.

Connecting common values between a sports organization and its audiences can create strong bonds and reinforce a sense of belonging. This allows both the organization and its audience to satisfy their needs and achieve their goals more effectively. On one hand, commercial needs carry greater weight for the sports organization, whereas for the public, the value offered by the organization—such as experiences, emotions, products and services, sporting success, identity, culture, and tradition—is paramount. This will vary depending on the specific type of audience involved.

Conclusions

Overall, the importance of transmitting values in the communication of sports organizations has been demonstrated. Creating bonds that foster identity, such as human values between organizations and their audiences, is of vital importance for both parties. The positive perception of strategic value-based communication by audiences can lead to tangible benefits for organizations. Value-based advertising communication positively influences the behavioral intent of fans in Mexican football clubs. The transmission and identification of values can positively impact the economic, social, cultural, and sporting interests of a society.

Finally, we can conclude that understanding values and effectively transmitting them through communication can help sports organizations achieve their goals, generating a significant impact on both their audiences and the organization itself.
